Privacy Policy

Favour Posting Bot · last updated 16 November 2026

Favour Posting Bot is a private tool used only by Favour Olayiwola to publish his own content to his own social media accounts (Instagram, Threads, X, LinkedIn and TikTok).

What it accesses

  • Basic profile information (account ID and display name) of the account owner, to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos and photos that the owner created.

What it does not do

  • It does not read messages, comments, followers or any other users' data.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not post on behalf of anyone other than the account owner.

Storage

Access tokens are stored encrypted as GitHub Actions secrets in a private repository and are used only to publish the owner's posts. No user data is stored beyond the owner's own published content.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time from the connected app settings of each platform.
